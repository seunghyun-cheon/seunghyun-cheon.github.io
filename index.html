About PRECISE Lab

Pharmacotherapy & Real-world Evidence for Clinical Safety & Efficacy

The PRECISE Lab investigates the safety and effectiveness of pharmacotherapy using real-world healthcare data.


Our research integrates clinical pharmacy, pharmacoepidemiology, pharmacovigilance, and data science to generate evidence that supports safer and more effective medication use in clinical practice.

Research

Our research focuses on generating clinically meaningful evidence from real-world healthcare data.

Real-world Evidence

Evaluation of treatment effectiveness and safety using nationwide claims databases, electronic health records, and other real-world healthcare data.

Drug Safety & Pharmacovigilance

Detection and evaluation of adverse drug events using spontaneous reporting systems and population-based databases.

Clinical Pharmacotherapy

Investigation of clinically important questions related to medication use, treatment strategies, and patient outcomes.

Healthcare Data Science

Application of statistical methods, machine learning, and data science approaches to healthcare and medication data.
