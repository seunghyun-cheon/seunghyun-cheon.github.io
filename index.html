About

From healthcare data
to clinical evidence.

Our goal is to generate evidence that helps clinicians make safer and more effective decisions about pharmacotherapy.

PRECISE Lab conducts clinical and population-based research on medication safety, effectiveness, and treatment outcomes.

We use real-world healthcare databases, spontaneous adverse event reporting systems, electronic health records, and advanced analytical methods to address questions that arise in clinical practice.

What We Study

Research Themes

Our research focuses on clinically meaningful questions surrounding medication use and patient outcomes.

01

Pharmacotherapy

Evaluation of medication use, treatment strategies, and patient outcomes in clinical practice.

02

Drug Safety

Investigation of adverse drug events and clinically important safety signals across diverse populations.

03

Clinical Effectiveness

Comparative evaluation of treatment effectiveness using real-world clinical populations.

04

Real-world Evidence

Generation of evidence from routinely collected healthcare data to support clinical decision making.

How We Study

Data & Methods

We combine large-scale healthcare data with epidemiologic, statistical, and computational approaches.

Healthcare Claims

Nationwide and population-based claims databases.

Clinical Data

Electronic health records and patient-level clinical data.

Pharmacovigilance

Spontaneous reporting systems and drug safety databases.

Data Science

Statistical modeling, causal inference, and machine learning.
